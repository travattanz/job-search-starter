# Skills Ledger

The honesty layer. Nothing goes on a resume or into an application answer unless
it is under **✅ Has it**. Everything else is a gap — write the gap down, don't
launder it into a claim.

Every line carries the date it was confirmed. Anything older than a year that
matters, re-confirm rather than assume.

Legend:

- **✅ Has it** — real work behind it; would survive an interview question
- **⚠️ Rusty / partial** — used it once, or years ago, or only in a course.
  Usable in conversation, **not** on a resume as a skill
- **❌ Doesn't have it** — asked and answered no. Never claim it. Keeps the skill
  from asking twice
- **❓ Pending** — asked, no answer yet. Off the resume until answered

## How this gets used

- **Tailoring a resume:** only ✅ terms may be mirrored from a posting. A posting
  keyword with no ✅ backing goes in the application folder's `notes.md` under
  gaps, and nowhere else.
- **Screening answers:** same rule. "Familiar with" is still a claim.
- **New skill comes up:** ask in the batched question round, then record it here
  with today's date. A skill stays off the resume until it's ✅.
- **Interview prep:** the ⚠️ and ❌ lists are the questions to expect. That's the
  useful reason to keep them.
